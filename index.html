RAPTOR CUSTOM FABWORX is a dedicated metal fabrication business operating from Vosloorus, Gauteng, South Africa. We transform raw steel and structural metals into custom-built components, architectural assemblies, security metalwork, and heavy-duty fabricated solutions.

Every commission begins with a disciplined focus on workmanship, accurate cutting, clean welding, and direct alignment with your functional and dimensional requirements. Whether producing a single bespoke piece or fabricating structural steel elements, our focus remains constant: reliable execution and lasting structural integrity.
